Introducing the Fudi app

The Fudi app keeps all your favorite recipes in your pocket. Search by ingredients, cuisine or cooking time and save the dishes you want to try later.

Make a shopping list in one tap, switch on the step by step mode while cooking and set timers right from the recipe. The app works even without the internet, so your recipes are always at hand: in the kitchen, in the shop or in the country house.

Download the app for free and get new seasonal recipes from our cooks every week. The best recipes of our users get into the weekly top and win prizes.
